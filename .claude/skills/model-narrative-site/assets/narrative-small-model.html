PT1 · VE1 · VE2 · PL1 · PT2 · PL2

It starts with a point and two directions

An origin at (0, 0, 0), Up along Z and Across along Y. Two planes hang off them: the plate plane through the origin, normal to Up, and the rib plane 16 mm across, normal to Across.

Nothing solid exists yet. These are the references every later feature is measured from.

SK1 · Sketch · Plate outline

A sketch that only knows its own plane

The plate outline is drawn in the plate plane's own u and v. Three lines and a 50 mm nose arc close the outline, and a 16 mm bolt hole, a 22 mm eye and a 70 mm slot become holes.

Four relations hold it: front and back horizontal, the side vertical, front parallel to back. The solver closes the chain and nests the holes into a single face.

NU1 · EX1 · Extrude · Plate

Pushed up by a number, not a drag

The extrude takes its distance from NU1, Plate thickness: 14 mm. That link is a reference stored in the document, so when the number changes, the plate changes with it.

PL2 · SK2 · Sketch · Rib section

A second sketch, standing up

The rib plane is vertical, so the same kind of drawing becomes a profile: a 210 mm foot, a sloped back and a 70 mm head. One relation keeps the foot parallel to the head.

EX2 · Extrude · Rib

14 mm across, normal to the profile

This extrude has a plain 14 mm typed into it rather than a link. That difference shows up later: when the plate thickness changes, the rib stays the same.

BO1 · Boolean · Union

Two solids, one body

A Boolean union fuses the plate and the rib where they meet. Two overlapping shells become one closed solid, with new edges where the rib lands on the plate.

FI1 · Fillet · Broken edges

Every edge, broken at 2 mm

The last feature takes the whole body and rounds every edge at 2 mm, the way a machinist deburrs a part. It's the most expensive step in the tree.

NU1 · setParameter

Change one number, rebuild four features

As you scroll, the plate thickness steps through 6, 10, 14, 20 and 28 mm. Each value was sent to the kernel on this page, which re-executed only what depends on it: NU1 → EX1 → BO1 → FI1. The sketches, planes and rib were skipped.
